How messages reach Pocket
Apple keeps the SMS inbox closed to apps. These are the routes that are actually possible on iOS/web today.
Paste what you copied Live in this app
Copy a bank message, then open Add entry — Pocket offers a "Use this" suggestion the moment your clipboard looks like a bank text. This is real, and works right now.
Share a message Needs a native share target
On a native iOS app, holding a bank text and tapping Share → Pocket would land it here parsed. This web app can't register as a share target, so use Paste instead for now.
Shortcuts automation Needs a native app / URL scheme
Apple's Shortcuts can watch for a message from a chosen sender and hand the text to an app that registers a URL scheme. A browser-only PWA can't receive that handoff, so this needs a native build to be real.
Bank email alerts Needs a mail-receiving backend
Forwarding statement emails to a private Pocket address would need a server that receives mail and parses it — this app has no backend, so this route isn't built.
Whatever the route, parsing happens on this device and every entry waits for your confirmation before it's added.
